What we do

Leafwise is a plant encyclopedia with a personal twist. Look up a plant to see how much sun and water it needs, then add it to your own garden. We remind you when it's time to water and let you log how much it has grown.

How to use

  1. Search for a plant on the Explore page.
  2. Open its details to see sunlight, watering and a short description.
  3. Create an account or log in, then add the plant to your garden.
  4. Check My garden for watering reminders, and log its height as it grows.
